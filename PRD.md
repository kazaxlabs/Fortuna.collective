# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Project: Fortuna Collective

### 1. Executive Summary
**Fortuna Collective** is a distinguished professional networking platform designed for elite members across high-value niches (Sales, Finance, Real Estate, Law, etc.). Unlike generic social networks, Fortuna emphasizes exclusivity, strategic professionalism, and a distinctive "Tactile Strategic" interface (Antigravity Design System).

### 2. Objectives & Mission
- **Exclusivity**: Provide a gated environment for verified professionals.
- **Strategic Networking**: Enable users to share "Briefs" and discover "Core Nodes" in their relevant niches.
- **Resource Security**: Offer a secure "Vault" for professional collateral and asset sharing.
- **Agent-Assisted Moderation**: Use AI-driven auditing to maintain the quality of the network.

### 3. Key Stakeholders
- **Strategic Architect (Admin)**: Oversees system operations, performs manual audits, and authorizes agent action proposals.
- **Core Node (Member)**: High-value individuals seeking to transmit knowledge and expand their strategic network.

### 4. Functional Requirements

#### 4.1 Onboarding & Identity
- Members must complete a multi-step onboarding process to define their "Niche" and professional identity.
- Identity attributes include: *Asset I Bring*, *Connection I Seeking*, and *Primary Field*.

#### 4.2 The Feed (Transmission Center)
- Chronological updates filtered by **Pillar Channels** (Niche-specific feeds).
- High-level "Announcements" and "Rules" curated by Strategic Architects.
- Multimedia support for professional briefs (images/collateral).

#### 4.3 Secure Channels (Messages)
- End-to-end direct messaging between synchronized nodes.
- "Correspondence" history with real-time updates.

#### 4.4 The Vault (Resource Library)
- Hierarchical file management system for professional documents.
- Support for folder structures and cloud-synced assets.

#### 4.5 Founder Control (Admin Dashboard)
- Real-time audit trails of suspicious activity.
- Authorization queue for agent-proposed actions (Bans, Holds, Expulsions).
- System-wide broadcast capabilities.

### 5. Interaction Design (UX)
- **Antigravity Interface**: A neumorphic design system that uses light and shadow to create a high-stakes, hardware-inspired tactical feel.
- **Native-Feel Mobile Navigation**: Optimized for iOS devices with safe-area awareness and custom haptic-style feedback.

### 6. Success Metrics
- **Node Sync Rate**: Average number of meaningful connections per member.
- **Transmission Quality**: Sentiment and engagement analysis of niche-specific briefs.
- **Audit Efficiency**: Response time to agent-flagged integrity violations.
